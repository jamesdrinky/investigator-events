--- INTRO ---
Last week was the season opener and it delivered. WAD ran its congress in
Cannes from the 1st to the 6th, the CII held its AGM in Prague across the
same days, and the SNARP AGM followed in Cannes on the Wednesday. Between
them, that is a good part of the profession in two cities in one week.

If you were at any of the three, there is one thing we would ask of you.
Leave a review. There are sixteen reviews on the entire site — sixteen, for a
calendar that now runs to eighty-five events. Most of us still pick where to
spend a training budget on a guess and a message to someone we know. A rating
and two honest lines fixes that faster than anything else we could build. The
three events are waiting at the bottom of this email and it takes under a
minute.

The other news is that the app is now on Android. It went live on Google Play
at the weekend, so if you have been waiting on it, it is there — same account,
same calendar, and you can leave that review from your phone on the way home.

This week Atlanta takes two. Secure Horizons opens on Saturday the 12th and
GSX follows in the same city on the 14th, with the ACFE's Africa conference
running in Johannesburg across those same three days. FALI-U is in Tampa on
the 16th, the D.A.CH Forum opens in Lochau on the 17th, and SPI holds a
dinner-meeting in Brooklyn that same evening.

If you are travelling to any of them, mark yourself as attending before you
go. It is still the quickest way to find out who else will be in the room.
