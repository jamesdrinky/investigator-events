--- INTRO ---
Cannes and Prague are done. WAD ran six days in Cannes, the CII held its AGM
in Prague across the same week, and the SNARP AGM followed on the Wednesday.

If you were at any of them, there is a review box on each event page. Two
lines is plenty, and it is worth more to whoever is weighing up next year
than anything we could write.

This week Atlanta takes two: Secure Horizons on the 12th, GSX on the 14th.
The ACFE's Africa conference runs in Johannesburg across the same days, then
FALI-U in Tampa, the D.A.CH Forum in Lochau, and an SPI dinner in Brooklyn on
the 17th.

The app is on Android as of this weekend.
