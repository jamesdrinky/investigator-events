## How to record on iPhone

1. Settings → Control Centre → add **Screen Recording** if it isn't there
2. Swipe down for Control Centre → tap the ⏺ record button → 3-2-1
3. Do the shot
4. Tap the red pill at the top → **Stop**
5. It saves to Photos → **AirDrop to the Mac**

Record **one clip per shot**, not all seven in a take. Fluff one, redo just that one.

## Before you record

- [ ] Focus / Do Not Disturb ON — a notification banner ruins a take
- [ ] Battery healthy, decent signal (the status bar is in every frame)
- [ ] Signed in, profile photo showing
- [ ] Matthew Spaier: just frame him out of shot — do NOT change the data
- [ ] Walk all 7 shots once without recording, to find the pacing

## Pacing

There's no cursor to follow on a phone, so **let each screen sit still for a full second** before you tap,
and scroll slowly — about half the speed that feels natural. Fast scrolling is the phone equivalent of a
darting cursor: the viewer can't read what went past. Everything else is fixable in the edit; pacing isn't.
