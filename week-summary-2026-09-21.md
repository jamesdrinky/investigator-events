## Associations

**Events can now be run by more than two associations.** Previously the site
only had room for one association and one co-host, so joint events were
described in the text and linked to nobody. The first D.A.CH Forum is the
example — it ran under the patronage of BUDEG, ÖDV, FSPD and SFPP, and now
links to all four. Each of them shows the event on their own page.

**WALI added** — the Washington Association of Legal Investigators, 220+
members, founded 1997. They have a full page with their logo and details.

**New: FSPD, SFPP & IKD AGM**, Einsiedeln, Switzerland, June 2027 — a
three-way joint AGM, and the first event to use the new multi-association
support.

**Associations now appear in site search.** Searching an association by name
or acronym previously returned nothing unless an event happened to mention
it, so the association pages were effectively unreachable from search.

## Newsletter

Three issues went out on schedule — 7, 14 and 21 September, to 214 subscribers
with no failures.

**Sign-up is now on far more of the site**: the homepage hero, every event
page, the associations index, and the member dashboard. It removes itself
automatically for anyone already subscribed, so nobody is asked twice.

**Anyone who subscribes mid-week now gets that week's issue immediately**
rather than waiting up to six days.

## What's working

Review numbers went from 16 to 40 over the last fortnight, almost entirely
driven by the newsletter asking. WAD Cannes has 11 and the CII AGM has 9 —
real write-ups from named attendees, which is the part of the calendar we
cannot produce ourselves.

## Fixes worth knowing about

- **The newsletter sign-up page was close to unusable on a phone** — the form
  sat behind the keyboard. Fixed, along with the same problem on every other
  form on the site.
- **The homepage was showing events that had already finished**, including
  conferences from April and May, while next week's were pushed off the list.
- **Consent handling**: the newsletter checkbox on the sign-up form had been
  pre-ticked since April. That is not valid consent under GDPR, so it is now
  unticked, and we keep a proper record of who was asked and what they said.
