## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Investigator Events is the dedicated network and events platform for professional
investigators, fraud examiners, corporate security specialists, and the wider
investigation community.

Discover what's happening in your field, connect with verified peers, and never
miss an important industry event again.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Browse the investigator events calendar — conferences, training, webinars and
  networking sessions from associations and organisers around the world.

• Get a weekly digest of new and upcoming events tailored to the investigation
  profession, so the important dates come to you.

• Build your professional profile — add your firm, specialisation and bio, and
  appear in the member directory.

• Connect with other investigators, send private messages, and grow a trusted
  professional network.

• Follow associations and organisations to stay on top of the events and updates
  that matter to you.

• Join the community — take part in discussions, post assistance requests, and
  share knowledge with peers who understand the work.

• List your own event and reach a focused, professional audience of investigators.

WHO IT'S FOR

Private investigators, corporate investigators, fraud examiners, due diligence
and OSINT professionals, corporate security teams, and the associations that
serve them.

A FOCUSED, PROFESSIONAL COMMUNITY

Investigator Events is built for people who work in investigation. It's not a
general social network — it's a focused space to find events, find people, and
stay current in a profession where the right connection and the right information
make all the difference.

Download Investigator Events and stay connected to your profession.
