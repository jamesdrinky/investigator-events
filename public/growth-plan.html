Built this week — click anything to demo it

Four things that work while we sleep. Nothing here needs Mike's time.

The weekly sweepBuilt

Every night the system fetches every association and conference page we watch and compares it with the night before — costs nothing to run. Pages that changed float to the top of the admin panel with the new date-mentioning lines already pulled out. Spot an event, click Draft, approve — it's on the calendar.

Why it matters: the calendar is the product, and we were adding 30 events a month in May vs 2 in August. Checking every site used to be the chore; now unchanged sites need zero attention, so the weekly sweep is minutes. 42 sources now monitored (every association website we know, discovered automatically) — it already caught TALI's annual conference announcement on its first pass.

On the call: which other associations and conference sites should we watch? Mike's list here is gold.

Live calendar subscriptionsBuilt

Anyone can now add our events to their own Google, Apple or Outlook calendar with one click — the whole calendar, one country, or one association. It updates itself forever.

Why it matters: once an investigator subscribes, every event we list lands in their personal calendar automatically. They never need to remember to visit the site — we're just there.

The widget — their events, on their site, powered by usBuilt

Each association embeds a live events section showing their own events — their colours, compact or full layout, resizes itself, with a "Subscribe" button that puts their calendar in members' pockets. One line of code, maintains itself forever.

Why it matters: their website runs on our data. Every association that embeds it is structurally dependent on our calendar being right — and a permanent referral source.

The Association Event ConsoleBuilt

The big one: each association's own admin gets a console where they add an event once. After our verification it appears simultaneously on their website widget, our global calendar, and every member's subscribed calendar. Edit and removal requests come through us too.

Why it matters: associations stop maintaining their own events pages and run them through us instead. They get an easier, better-looking system for free; we get the market data and become the rails their events run on. We grant and revoke manager access from the admin panel.

Date clash checker + date watchBuilt

Organisers planning a conference check their dates against every event we know about — including near misses the week either side. They can leave an email and we alert them if anything clashing gets announced later.

Why it matters: this makes organisers depend on our calendar being complete — and every check hands us a warm organiser lead with their event name and dates.
