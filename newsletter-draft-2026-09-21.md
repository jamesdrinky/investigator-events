--- INTRO ---
Thursday is the busiest day of the month. TALI opens in San Antonio and runs
to Saturday, and NALI Region II meets in Detroit with MCPI across the same
two days. PBSA follows in Arlington on Sunday the 27th. The Advanced
Intelligence Tradecraft Intensive opens in Sydney today.

Seven people have already marked themselves as going to TALI. If you are
travelling to any of these, mark yourself too — it is the quickest way to
find out who else will be in the room, and the only way we can tell you when
someone you know signs up.

October opens with CODEGA in La Coruña on the 3rd, the OSMOSIS Expo in Reston
on the 6th, and both CALI in Cathedral City and the Component Method course
in Denver on the 8th.

Lochau, Brooklyn and Johannesburg all wrapped last week. If you were at one,
the review box is open at the bottom of this email.
